Questions people ask

How long does a website take?

A simple one-page site is usually ready in one to two weeks, and multi-page sites take longer. I give you a timeline in the quote, based on how quickly we get your content and feedback.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business or organization name, a short description, contact details and any logo or photos you have. If you don't have everything, we'll work out the gaps together.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

Yes, to have a live address like yourname.com. I'll explain the options in plain language and help you set them up, so you stay the owner of your site.

Can you improve a website I already have?

Yes. I can redesign it, make it work properly on phones, speed it up and clean up the content so visitors find what they need.

Can you work with my organization's timeline or event date?

Yes. Tell me the date when we talk, and I'll plan the work backwards from it so the site is live before people need it.

How do I pay?

We agree the price up front. Payment is split into a deposit to start and the balance at launch. Details are written in your quote.
